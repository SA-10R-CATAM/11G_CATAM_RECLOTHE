About Us

ReClothe is a business built by 5 young entrepreneurs who wanted to integrate what they, as a generation, experienced and what past generations have been striving for; an eco-friendly way to upcycle clothing. It was inspired by these youthful minds wanting a sustainable future, while tending to people's convenience. We purchase vintage apparel from customers and refurbish them into quality unique to the previous pieces of clothing.

We offer services that help rid customers of wasted space, assist them in supporting local businesses, and aid them in identifying and recognising how climate change is a real problem. We keep the values of preservation, distinction, and knowledge, aiming to uphold those traits in what we do as a business.
